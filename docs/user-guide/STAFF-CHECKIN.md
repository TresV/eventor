# Staff Check-in

The Check-in page is the screen your event-day team uses to validate tickets.

## Recommended setup

1. Create or edit a Staff Check-in page.
2. Add the `Check-in` widget.
3. Keep the page out of public navigation.
4. Assign the Staff role only to operational users.
5. Test QR and manual-code flows before the event.

## Related setup

See `STAFF-ROLE-SETUP.md` to configure who can reach the page and where `Visit Site` redirects Staff users.
